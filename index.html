Selected Work

Projects

Seismic Activity Detector

Built a compact seismic-analysis pipeline with MiniSEED/GeoCSV/XML ingestion, adaptive preprocessing, and Conv1D/LSTM models for event/anomaly detection. Designed to save ~657 analyst hours (≈82 human-days).

ESPCN++ Super Resolution

Created a lightweight ESPCN-based ×6 SR model with skip connections and Single-Pixel Attention. Auto-enhances ~40 catalog images/day, potentially saving ~1 human-day by removing manual SR recaptures.

Medical Bot

Developed a clinical chatbot using NLP (spaCy/Transformers), FastAPI, and LangChain. Parses prescriptions and applies dosing rules. Designed to automate 200–2,000 prescriptions/day, saving 3–6 min each.

Chatter — On-Chain Messaging

Built an on-chain chat DApp with an event-based Solidity contract for low-cost messaging and wallet identity via EIP-4337 account abstraction, plus an indexing layer for real-time message streaming.
